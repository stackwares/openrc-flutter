OpenRC is an open-source dashboard for RevenueCat. It answers the question you ask ten times a day: how are sales going?

Sign in with your RevenueCat account and OpenRC works out the answers on your device.

• Every sale, as it happens: today's transactions with app icons, products, countries and stores, a running total against yesterday, and a seven-day strip to step back through the week.
• What each sale is: new subscriptions, renewals, trials, conversions, refunds and cancellations, counted and filterable.
• Revenue you'll actually receive: optionally net of store fees, with your own fee rates, in any of 100 display currencies.
• When you'll get paid: upcoming App Store and Google Play payouts.
• Where the money comes from: breakdowns by store, new vs. renewals, top apps and top countries.
• The charts that matter: MRR, ARR, revenue, subscriptions, trials, users, churn and more, over any date range.
• At a glance: home screen widgets.

Private by design: your password goes straight to RevenueCat and is never seen or stored by OpenRC. No OpenRC server holds your data. Crash reports and usage stats are anonymous and can be turned off in Settings.

OpenRC is free, open source (MIT) and not affiliated with RevenueCat, Inc.
