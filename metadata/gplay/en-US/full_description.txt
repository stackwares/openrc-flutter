OpenRC is the mobile RevenueCat dashboard for app developers who want subscription and in-app purchase metrics in their pocket—not only at a desk.

Sign in with your RevenueCat account. OpenRC loads your data on device and helps you answer the question you ask all day: how are sales going?

WHY OPENRC
• Built for quick checks between meetings, launches, and ad runs
• Free and open source (MIT): https://github.com/stackwares/openrc-flutter
• Not affiliated with RevenueCat, Inc.

LIVE SALES & TRANSACTIONS
• Today’s transactions with app icons, products, countries, and stores
• Running total vs. yesterday plus a 7-day strip to scan the week
• Filter new subscriptions, renewals, trials, conversions, refunds, and cancellations

REVENUE & PAYOUTS
• Revenue and proceeds, optionally net of App Store and Google Play fees
• Your fee rates and 100+ display currencies
• Upcoming payout dates on Apple’s fiscal calendar and Google Play schedules

ANALYTICS & CHARTS
• MRR, ARR, revenue, subscriptions, trials, active users, and churn
• Pick any date range; compare stores, apps, and countries
• Top apps and top countries for the day or month to date

WIDGETS & PRIVACY
• Home screen widgets for at-a-glance metrics on Android
• Your password goes directly to RevenueCat; OpenRC does not store it
• No OpenRC backend on native Android—requests go to RevenueCat
• Optional anonymous crash and usage analytics; disable anytime in Settings

OpenRC is free. Use it alongside the official RevenueCat web dashboard whenever you need depth—use OpenRC when you need speed.

Source code: https://github.com/stackwares/openrc-flutter
